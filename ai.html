AI use

A note from the developer on where artificial intelligence is at work while you use the reader. There is one such service: Jev, from TypeSafe AI. It is a classifier. It is given a question and a fixed list of possible answers, and it picks one. It writes nothing you read.

What it does

  • Sorts stories. Each new story is labelled with a topic and, where one applies, a place, chosen from a fixed list. The Topic and Place filters and a story's tags run on these labels.
  • Works out what you meant. When you add an outlet by typing its name, it picks the most likely match from a short list, so a misspelling still finds the outlet.
  • Chooses among an outlet's feeds. Large outlets publish dozens. It picks out the main ones to offer you.
  • Finds the support link. It picks which link on an outlet's home page is the one for donating or subscribing.

What it is sent

  • To sort a story: the outlet's name, the headline, the summary, the first 600 characters of the story and the publisher's own categories. All of it is already public.
  • When you add an outlet: the words you typed into the lookup box, the outlet's web address, and the names and addresses of its feeds and home page links.
  • Never: your screen name, email, password, what you read or saved, your friends, or your messages. No request says who is asking.

What it does not do

  • It does not write, summarise or rewrite anything. Headlines and story text are the publishers' own words, shown as published.
  • It does not choose or rank what you see. The list is in order of time, from the outlets you follow. Nothing is recommended and nothing is personalised.
  • It does not read the Gossip Column. Messages are sealed in your browser before they are sent, and neither the server nor any AI service can open them.
  • It is not a chatbot, and this project does not use anything you do in the reader to train one.

Where it can be wrong

Topic and place labels are a best guess and are sometimes wrong. A mislabelled story is still in your list: it is only filed under the wrong tag. If the service cannot be reached, stories arrive without labels and everything else works as usual.
